# Jober — Product positioning

## What Jober is

Jober is an **honest, you-in-the-loop** job application assistant. It helps you research roles, tailor materials, fill forms with your consent, and track every attempt — while you stay in control of what gets submitted and when.

## What Jober is not

Jober is **not** a spray-and-pray auto-applier. It does not:

- Mass-apply to jobs you did not choose
- Bypass CAPTCHA, login walls, or employer security controls
- Hide automation from you or from employers
- Auto-submit by default — `review_before_submit` is the baseline; `auto_submit` is opt-in and clearly disclosed

## Core value

| Pillar | Promise |
|--------|---------|
| **Quality** | Tailored cover letters **and** human-approved resume variants, plus form fills grounded in your profile and the specific role |
| **Matching** | Discover and import high-signal jobs with explainable fit — build the perfect list before you apply |
| **Tracking** | Full run history, artifacts, and batch visibility — know what happened on each application |
| **Control** | Human checkpoints for sensitive fields, auth challenges, and final **Approve / Send** |
| **Integrity** | Respect site terms; sensitive EEO/veteran fields require explicit user entry |

## Differentiation

Mass auto-submit tools optimize for volume and concealment. Jober optimizes for **signal**: fewer, better-targeted applications with an audit trail you can trust. That integrity is a feature — especially as ATS vendors and employers tighten bot detection.

## Commercial model (Mission 15)

- **Free**: limited batch size and monthly runs — enough to evaluate the product honestly
- **Pro**: higher limits, usage dashboard, Stripe-managed subscription
- **Tenant isolation**: your jobs, documents, and artifacts are never visible to other accounts
- **Compliance**: export all your data; delete your account data with confirmation; audit log for sensitive actions

## Responsible use

Users apply only to jobs they select, follow each site's terms of service, and complete security challenges themselves. Jober pauses and hands off — it never attempts evasion.
